Crowdfind

A community driven guide website for open-world games. This website helps players discover puzzles, quests, and achievements, while letting the community be the first to unlock and document new discoveries.

Date Feb – Apr 2026
Role Web Developer & UI Designer
Tools Figma & Canva
Team Julia, Giulian, Daniel
The Problem

Players get stuck. They need a better way to find answers.

When players get stuck on a puzzle, quest, or achievement in an open-world game, they have to scour multiple platforms just to find the answer. There is no single website that combines community knowledge in a structured, trustworthy way.

Answers are scattered

Answers are spread across different websites like Reddit, Discord, YouTube and various wikis. This makes it difficult for players to find reliable information in one place.

Quality is inconsistent

User-made guides vary wildly in accuracy, completeness and clarity.

Discoveries go undocumented

New discoveries can go undocumented for days, leaving players frustrated and looking for answers elsewhere.

Developers have no channel

Developers have no way to contribute official guidance directly to the community in a structured, accessible way.

The Solution

One platform where community knowledge is structured and trustworthy.

Crowdfind is a web platform that pairs with a desktop app that tracks real-time discoveries. When a player discovers a new achievement or solves a puzzle, the companion app automatically detects the unlock and prompts them to document it on Crowdfind. Developers can also contribute official content to the platform to help users out.

Syncs via the desktop app

The companion app pairs with the website and automatically detects in-game achievements as they're unlocked. It prompts users to document and share their discovery with the community in real time.

Being first matters

Players who are first to document a new achievement are credited on the platform. This creates a community incentive to explore, discover and share findings quickly.

Developer-endorsed content

Game developers can pre-populate Crowdfind with official tutorials and puzzle guides to help users navigating their game for the first time.

Audience

Designed for two distinct users.

Players

Open-world game enthusiasts who encounter puzzles, quests and lore they want help with. Many of them also want to be recognized for being the first to discover and document something new in the community.

Game developers

Studios and indie developers building open-world games who want to give their playerbase a reliable hub for community knowledge.

My Role

Responsible for the Figma prototype and website code.

Within our three-person team, I led the UI design and front-end development. I built the Figma prototype to show for the class on the last week of the semester. Julia and Daniel handled visual branding and the video pitch respectively, while all three of us contributed to the overall landing page design direction.
