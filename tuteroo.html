About

Fast, transparent, and hassle-free tutor booking.

The Tuteroo mobile app is designed to allow tutors to list their services while enabling parents to search for and book sessions with them.

Pain Points

Four things that make booking a tutor hard.

01

Parents might have trouble feeling confident using an app to book tutors. Verification checks, background checks, and user reviews help combat this.

02

Managing real-time tutor availability and preventing double bookings or last-minute cancellations is challenging.

03

Ensuring clear pricing, secure transactions, and refund policies can be complex and may impact user trust.

04

Keeping parents and tutors engaged, encouraging repeat bookings, and preventing churn can be difficult without strong user incentives and features.

Problem & Solution

One platform for parents and tutors.

The Problem

Parents often struggle to find qualified tutors and determine if they are available in their local area.

Tutors need a reliable, reputable platform to list their services, one that has consistent user engagement.

The Solution

Provide a central platform where parents can easily search for tutoring services.

Give tutors a convenient space to list and promote their services.
